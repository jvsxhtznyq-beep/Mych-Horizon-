# Template: Invention Concept

The Inventor's deliverable: a framed problem, mapped prior art, distinct concepts with honest ratings, and a prototype plan aimed at the riskiest assumption. Method: [inventor.md](../modes/inventor.md); ratings: [feasibility-scale.md](../references/feasibility-scale.md).

## How to fill it

- Concepts must differ in mechanism; merge reskinned variations.
- Every concept gets a feasibility level with a reason and its riskiest assumption.
- Write prototype success and kill criteria before any prototype exists.
- Report negative results as plainly as positive ones.
