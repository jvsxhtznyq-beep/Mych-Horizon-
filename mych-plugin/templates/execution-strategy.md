# Template: Execution Strategy

The coordinator's orientation record for Tier 3 work: the nine orientation questions answered compactly, plus the approach that follows from them. Share a condensed version (objective, approach, phases, approval points, open decisions) with the owner; keep deliberation out of it.

## How to fill it

- One line per item where possible. If a section needs a paragraph, the task may need more discovery first.
- Every success criterion gets a verification method now, before implementation.
- Mark each gap with its action: inspect, research, assume (state the assumption) or ask.
- Update the strategy when a checkpoint changes the plan; note the date of the change.
