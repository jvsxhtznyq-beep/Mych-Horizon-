How to play

  • ← → or A D: run
  • Space, Z, ↑ or W: jump (hold for higher jumps)
  • Esc or P: pause
  • Gamepad: stick or d-pad to run, A to jump, Start to pause. Touch: on-screen buttons.
  • Land on slimes, bats and spore turrets to defeat them. Avoid spikes and spores.
  • Glowmoss orbs give points; collect them all for a bonus. Heart crystals heal you.
  • Touch a glowing lamp to save your place. Reach the green door to finish the cavern.
